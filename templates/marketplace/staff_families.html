Group products into families

A family is one product that comes in several sizes, flavours or packs, for example Kakira Sugar 1KG, 2KG and 5KG. Customers then see one card with "3 sizes" instead of three separate products.

How this works

  • These are suggestions from the product names. Nothing changes until you press Group.
  • Check the family name and each label (you can edit them), untick anything that does not belong, then press Group these.
  • Press Not a family if the products are really different. The suggestion will not come back.
  • You can Undo right after grouping. Later changes can be made in the admin (Products, Parent group and Variant label).
  • "Sizes" suggestions are usually right. "Check these" suggestions only share the first words of the name, so look carefully.
