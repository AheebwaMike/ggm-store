Using the code on your own poster

  • SVG is best for designers: it stays sharp at any size. PNG is about 2000 pixels wide, enough for a poster up to A3.
  • Keep the white margin around the code, keep it dark on white, and do not stretch, recolour or crop it.
  • Make it at least 10 cm wide for a gate or corridor poster. A good rule is one-tenth of the scanning distance.
  • Always scan the final printed poster with two different phones before printing many copies.
  • Choosing a placement (gate, corridor…) lets you see which spots get scanned. The apartment stays the same.
